## Choice

ICML 2027 and ICLR 2027 do not have an open workshop paper call. The soonest confirmed biomedical conference date is CHIL on 3 February 2027, but its template is not published yet and the length rule is a minimum of 8 pages as well as a maximum of 10. AAAI-27’s program date of 20 November 2026 is sooner, and the official program page does not name a biomedical negative-results workshop with a template.

TMLR is the venue used for `paper/tmlr/`. The acceptance criteria say a paper need not be novel or state of the art, that claims must match the evidence, and that a reproducibility study with a lesson for other researchers can be of interest. That matches a narrow null about a sparse biological channel. Submissions are open now, which is earlier than CHIL, and the style file is published. DMLR is also open, but it is single-blind and its scope is data-centric machine learning; this paper’s main claim is an evaluation result, not a new dataset.

The previous ICBINB draft is left in `paper/icbinb/`.
